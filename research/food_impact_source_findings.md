# Food impact source findings

## Ecosperity report

Source: Environmental Impact of Key Food Items in Singapore, October 2019, published by Temasek, Deloitte, and Singapore Institute of Manufacturing Technology.

The report uses a life cycle assessment approach and considers greenhouse gas emissions, energy consumption, and water consumption. It studies 13 food item groups consumed in Singapore, including beef, mutton, pork, chicken, duck, egg, fish, other seafood, fruits, leafy vegetables, other vegetables, rice, and wheat.

The report states that the food estimate considers production, processing, and transportation stages, and also considers food loss along those stages. Its food consumption estimate is based on import quantity, locally produced quantity, and export quantity divided by population.

The executive summary states that environmental impact is highest for meat, especially pork and mutton, while rice has the highest water consumption per kilogram basis. It also explains that air transport can have a large effect for some imported chilled foods, while sourcing, transport distance, energy sources, and local production can change the result.

## Product use rule

This report should be used as one reference source for ingredient factors, not as a universal exact value for every plated meal. The application should preserve the report units, record the source and factor scope, and show an uncertainty range when converting ingredient or portion estimates into a meal estimate.

## Required review safeguards

Image recognition must return likely dish labels and visible ingredients with confidence levels. The student must be able to correct the dish, remove an ingredient, change an amount, and choose a cooking or sourcing assumption before saving. If the image is unclear, the application must provide a manual or custom estimate path instead of pretending certainty.
