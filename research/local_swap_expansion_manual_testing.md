# Additional local Singapore sustainable swap options

## Coverage

Every supported local Singapore meal now offers at least two optional sustainable swap ideas. The ideas provide meal variety without stating a fixed carbon saving.

1. Chicken Rice includes a vegetable rice bowl and a version with Chinese greens and a smaller chicken portion.
2. Laksa includes a vegetable noodle soup and a tofu and vegetable laksa style noodle soup.
3. Chicken Biryani includes vegetable biryani and a plate with a smaller chicken portion and vegetable raita.
4. Roti Prata includes vegetable curry and dhal with cucumber.
5. Nasi Lemak includes tempeh with cucumber and a version with egg, cucumber, and less fried chicken.
6. Char Kway Teow includes vegetable bee hoon with tofu and kway teow with tofu and leafy greens.
7. Hokkien Mee includes vegetable noodle soup and a version with extra greens and a smaller seafood portion.
8. Pork Wanton Mee includes vegetable wanton noodle soup and mushroom with tofu wanton mee.
9. Fishball Noodle Soup includes tofu and vegetable soup plus vegetable yong tau foo with noodles.
10. Mee Soto includes tofu and vegetable mee soto plus bean sprouts and tofu mee soto.
11. Vegetarian Bee Hoon includes an extra vegetable version plus tofu and leafy greens.
12. Beef Rendang Rice includes tempeh and vegetable rendang plus a smaller beef portion with vegetable sides.

## Safeguards

Every card states the original meal, alternative, ingredients, explanation, and limitation. The app does not invent a carbon saving. Vegetarian, allergy, avoided ingredient, and cautious cultural preference filters continue to withhold unsuitable options.

## Manual testing steps

1. Open Chicken Rice and confirm two sustainable swap cards are shown.
2. Open a second local meal such as Laksa or Nasi Lemak and confirm two different cards are shown.
3. Turn on Vegetarian swap options only and confirm non vegetarian ideas are hidden.
4. Add tofu to Allergies or safety concerns and confirm tofu based ideas are withheld.
5. Enter a cultural preference without a verified tag and confirm no unsuitable swap is shown.

## Mobile and automated checks

Chicken Rice was checked at 390 pixel mobile width. Both cards remain readable and the original meal label is clear. The full automated suite passed 110 tests and the production build passed.
