# Local Singapore swap expansion verification

## Verified coverage

Each of the twelve supported local meals now has two sustainable swap ideas. This includes Chicken Rice, Laksa, Chicken Biryani, Roti Prata, Nasi Lemak, Char Kway Teow, Hokkien Mee, Pork Wanton Mee, Fishball Noodle Soup, Mee Soto, Vegetarian Bee Hoon, and Beef Rendang Rice.

## Verified safeguards

The swap filter still excludes options that do not meet vegetarian, allergy, avoided ingredient, or cautious cultural preference settings. Every option uses a limitation statement and does not show a calculated or fixed carbon saving.

## Verified mobile display

Chicken Rice was checked at 390 pixel width. Both sustainable swap cards showed the original meal, alternative, explanation, limitation, and View this option control without horizontal scrolling.

## Automated verification

The tests require at least two transparent options for each supported local meal. The complete suite passed 110 tests and the production build passed.
