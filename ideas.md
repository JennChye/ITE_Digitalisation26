# PlateFootprint Design Ideas

## Three design directions

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hawker Market Journal | A warm food diary inspired by Singapore hawker culture and fresh market produce. It feels personal, kind, and simple for students. | 0.07 |
| Study Garden | A calm campus notebook with leaf shapes and clear study cards. It makes carbon learning feel friendly and practical. | 0.04 |
| Earth Lab | A clean learning tool with strong data blocks and soft soil colours. It makes climate information easy to understand. | 0.09 |

## Chosen direction: Hawker Market Journal

### Design Movement

This design takes ideas from **editorial food journals** and **Singapore hawker culture**. It uses warm paper, food card labels, and simple hand drawn details.

### Core Principles

1. Make every carbon number easy to notice and understand.
2. Use friendly food language with no blame.
3. Give large touch areas for mobile use.
4. Build calm visual steps from meal choice to learning.

### Color Philosophy

Warm rice paper gives the app a welcoming base. Leaf green shows positive action. Dark forest text keeps facts clear. Chilli orange marks higher impact food without making it feel bad.

### Layout Paradigm

The page is a vertical food journal. A welcome note leads into a pinned impact panel, then a flowing stack of large dish cards. This avoids a fixed desktop grid and works naturally on a phone.

### Signature Elements

1. A bowl and leaf brand mark.
2. Food label cards with a coloured impact dot.
3. Small hand drawn leaf curves as section details.

### Interaction Philosophy

Cards respond with a small press movement. Tapping a dish opens a simple learning panel with its carbon score and one practical action.

### Animation

Cards softly appear from below when the page opens. Pressed buttons shrink slightly for quick feedback. All motion stays short and stops for users who prefer less motion.

### Typography System

Fraunces is used for warm food journal headings. Nunito Sans is used for clear body text and labels. Headings are bold and relaxed. Supporting text is simple and readable.

### Brand Essence

PlateFootprint helps ITE students in Singapore explore the climate impact of everyday meals in a clear and friendly way.

**Personality:** warm, practical, encouraging.

### Brand Voice

Headlines sound friendly and direct. Buttons use clear actions. Small notes explain that every choice is a learning step.

Example lines:

> Today’s meal has a story.

> Explore a lighter choice for your next meal.

### Wordmark and Logo

The logo is a simple rice bowl shape where one rising steam line becomes a leaf. The PlateFootprint wordmark uses a soft editorial serif beside the mark.

### Signature Brand Color

**Hawker Leaf Green** is a rich plant green that makes the app feel active, local, and hopeful.

## Style Decisions

1. Each dish card shows a visible score, coloured impact dot, and plain language impact note before it is opened.
2. Chilli orange is used only for higher impact signals, key notes, and small editorial details.
3. Rice paper cards, hawker labels, receipt notes, and hand drawn leaf curves are the main visual surfaces.
